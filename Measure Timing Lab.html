Two ways the Measure could work

Round robin. The Measure walks its slots in a fixed loop, one 3 second cast per slot. While the pet is still charmed, charm's slot is passed over. When the charm breaks, it waits until the loop comes back around to it, so the gap depends on where in the loop the break happened. More buffs means a longer loop and a longer worst case.

Refresh when low. The Measure only recasts a song when it is nearly out, and a broken charm joins the line immediately. Long gaps only happen when several buffs come due at the same moment and charm has to wait behind them.

Best fit so far: round robin where charm's slot still costs its 3 second beat, plus a small chance the charm breaks early. It is the only setup that reproduces all three of your results: under 3 seconds to the cast starting with charm alone, up to about 12 with 2 buffs, and anywhere from about 3 to 15 with 3 buffs. Those are the defaults below. Refresh when low can't produce anything past about 12, so your 16 second gap argues against it.
