Fight

An empty rotation with no instrument and attack off is the ~90s baseline: warrior swings + druid nukes only. Everything the bard adds is measured against that.

Instrument

Picking an instrument changes the scene. Its one flat benefit applies to every song; only Brass builds resonance.

Song rotation

Songs are sung back-to-back, 3s each (2.5s on Wind). Effects land at cast end and tick on the 6s heartbeat. Base holds 6 songs; Wind 7, String 8.

Medley

Write a composition in eight sections. Any section can hold a song or an action. Songs must also be on your song bar above, or the section is skipped. Only Chorus and the Perform stance leave a medley running — anything else drops you into jazz.
